🧠 Problem We Solve

  • Most users track spending but do not understand why overspending happens.
  • Traditional trackers show numbers only and miss behavioral context.
  • Budget control, motivation, and reporting are often scattered across tools.

⚡ Our Solution

  • Emotion + finance analytics reveals spending patterns behind the numbers.
  • An AI assistant provides personalized insights, recommendations, and quick actions.
  • Goals, challenges, and reports are unified in one connected platform.

Key Product Capabilities

AI Chat Assistant

Guides users with personalized financial insights and practical next steps.

Emotion Analytics

Visualizes happy, neutral, and sad spending patterns over time.

Goals & Budgets

Helps users set limits, track progress, and stay on plan.

Challenges & Achievements

Boosts motivation through gamified milestones and leaderboards.

Smart Reports

Exports clear PDF/Excel/HTML reports for analysis and sharing.

Multilingual Experience

Supports multiple languages for broader accessibility.

Technology Stack

  • React + TypeScript + Vite
  • NestJS + TypeORM
  • SQLite (MVP), with a scalable database path
  • Gemini AI integration
  • Rate limiting, validation, and monitoring

Product Roadmap

  • Strengthen production-grade security and authorization.
  • Scale the data layer for larger concurrent user traffic.
  • Expand mobile-first workflows and notifications.
  • Introduce AI-based spending forecasts and savings plans.

Expected Impact

EmotionFlow helps users move from passive tracking to active decision-making. By combining financial data with emotional context and AI guidance, the platform improves awareness, discipline, and long-term money habits for individuals and families.
